DURABLE POWER OF ATTORNEY FOR HEALTHCARE / HEALTHCARE PROXY

I, [CLIENT FULL NAME], residing in [STATE], appoint [PRIMARY HEALTHCARE
AGENT NAME] as my Healthcare Agent to make healthcare decisions for me if
I am unable to make them for myself. If my primary Agent is unable or
unwilling to serve, I appoint [ALTERNATE HEALTHCARE AGENT NAME] as
successor Agent.

ARTICLE I — GRANT OF AUTHORITY
My Agent shall have full authority to make healthcare decisions on my
behalf, including decisions about medical treatment, surgical procedures,
admission to or discharge from medical facilities, and access to my
medical records, subject to any limitations stated below.

ARTICLE II — LIMITATIONS ON AUTHORITY
[SPECIFY ANY LIMITATIONS, e.g. restrictions on withdrawal of life support,

ARTICLE III — END-OF-LIFE WISHES / LIVING WILL COORDINATION
[REFERENCE ANY SEPARATE LIVING WILL / ADVANCE DIRECTIVE, OR STATE WISHES
REGARDING LIFE-SUSTAINING TREATMENT].

ARTICLE IV — HIPAA AUTHORIZATION
My Agent is authorized to receive and review my protected health
information under HIPAA to the extent necessary to make informed
healthcare decisions on my behalf. [SEE ALSO SEPARATE HIPAA AUTHORIZATION
DOCUMENT].

ARTICLE V — EFFECTIVE DATE
This Healthcare Power of Attorney becomes effective upon a determination,
in accordance with [STATE] law, that I lack capacity to make my own
healthcare decisions.
