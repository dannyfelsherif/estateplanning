could act myself with respect to the powers described below. If my primary
Agent is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint [ALTERNATE AGENT NAME] as
successor Agent.

ARTICLE I — EFFECTIVE DATE
This Power of Attorney is effective [IMMEDIATELY / UPON DETERMINATION OF

My Agent shall have the power to act on my behalf regarding: banking and

personal property transactions; business operating transactions;
retirement plan transactions; tax matters; insurance and annuity
transactions; claims and litigation; and gift-giving as limited below.

ARTICLE III — GIFTING LIMITATIONS
My Agent may make gifts on my behalf only as follows: [SPECIFY LIMITS, e.g.
annual federal gift tax exclusion amount per recipient, or "no gifting
authority"].

ARTICLE IV — COMPENSATION
My Agent shall / shall not [SPECIFY] be entitled to reasonable compensation
for services rendered under this Power of Attorney.

ARTICLE V — REVOCATION
I reserve the right to revoke this Power of Attorney at any time by written
notice delivered to my Agent.
